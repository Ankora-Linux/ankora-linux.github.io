Ankora Linux 5.0 Features

AyazDE 5.0 unifies 20 built-in windows, 24 themes, four core architectures, allowlisted AI, and 559 packages.

Six highlights of the release

Default Bostan & 4 Desktop Faces

Opens with Bostan by default; switch immediately across Bakır, Cıvata, Arduvaz, and preserved 4.7 Classic.

Ankora AI Chat Bubble & 4 Modes

Sysadmin Inspect, System Doctor, Developer, and Desktop Agent modes pass 6 recent turns to local Ollama or cloud models.

Tauri 2.0 & WebKitGTK 4.1

Clean window management, 30-second lazy DOM cleanup, and bwrap-sandboxed webviews.

559 Packages & Classroom Ready

Unified APT, Flathub, and official repo alongside eta-touchdrv, Fatih Kalem 2, MEB cert tools, and CUPS/SANE.

24 System Themes & Dynamic Island

Full palette coherence with 19 wallpapers and an optional top telemetry/media pill.

Rollback-Safe Verified Updater

ayaz-update-helper validates SHA-256 and GPG signatures and restores the prior binary on failure.

Four AI Modes & Providers

Test the Rust terminal command gate live and filter highlighted packages from the 559-package catalog.

Four AI Modes & Providers

Passes the last 6 conversation turns to local Ollama (127.0.0.1:11434), Gemini 2.5 Flash, OpenAI, Groq, or OpenRouter.

Reads live RAM, CPU load, and root disk usage before suggesting safe actions.
